# ClearCart app surfaces (all pages)

Scope: welcome, setup, assistant (hero), store, check-your-order, receipt, error. Mode: Operate. Build path: code-led.
Audience: blind and low-vision shoppers; demoed to PayPal hackathon judges. User asked for "cutesy and blind people friendly" and chose Sticker sheet. Avoid: generic AI-chatbot look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Shopping with a cheerful little helper, where every cute sticker still reads at 8:1 or better. Refuses the grey chatbot and the clinical accessibility form.

OWN-WORLD: Kawaii sticker sheet. Strawberry pink header, pale pink dotted ground; candy stickers in mint, butter, lilac, sky, peach, each with a 3px plum (#2b1a2e) outline, white die-cut ring and soft lift. Fredoka for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for body. Puffy pill buttons in deep pink with plum text.

STORY: Basket the mascot keeps the shopper company; the shopper always sees and hears exactly what will be charged, and nothing moves without a typed PIN.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pink header (Basket + ClearCart, sandbox pill, nav pills). Left: Basket with account chips, the chat log as a white sticker holding mint (ClearCart) and butter (You) message stickers, input row below. Right: the butter "Pay £x" sticker with Basket peeking over its corner, read-back card, candy limit bar, four PIN bubbles, Pay/Cancel.

FORM: Sticker sheet, user-pinned direction, seed: none (unavailable). Signature move: Basket the mascot reacts to state (thinking, listening, excited, happy, worried, sleepy), always mirrored in text; PIN bubbles fill like candy.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
